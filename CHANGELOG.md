# Changelog

## 0.0.1-alpha — 2026-10-16

The first release.

- **Capture** with ⌥⇧S anywhere (a system hotkey; no Accessibility permission), or the Capture button or menu bar icon: drag a region, or press Space and click a window.
- **Frame:** nine backdrops (none, five gradients, three plain colors), padding, rounded corners and a soft shadow. Your style is remembered.
- **Mark up:** arrow, box, highlighter, redact (pixelate), numbered steps, text (Khmer shaped), crop; six colors; undo and redo.
- **↩ or ⌘C** copies the finished PNG and gets out of the way; **⌘S** saves a file.
- **Check for updates** downloads the next release from GitHub, checks its SHA-256 and restarts into it. rsnap never checks on its own.

Apple silicon, macOS 11 or later. Not notarized: right-click → Open the first time, or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/rsnap.app`.
